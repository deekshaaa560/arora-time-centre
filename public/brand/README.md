# Arora Time Centre — Brand Kit v1.0

The visual identity for Arora Time Centre: a watch dial, the family initial, and one point of champagne at the centre.

## The mark

A single hairline bezel, a fluted crown at 3 o'clock, and a Playfair Display **A** set inside a 60-tick minute track. The accent dot sits on the A's crossbar at the exact centre of the dial — a jewel, a pivot, and a small sunrise for the name *Arora*. The app tile uses 12 bold hour marks so the dial survives at favicon sizes.

## Palette

- Ink — `#0B0B0B`
- Cream — `#F2F0EA`
- Champagne — `#C9A24B` (dark surfaces)
- Deep champagne — `#A67C2E` (light surfaces)

The mark is one base colour plus a single accent. Champagne appears in the minute track, the centre dot, and the `TIME CENTRE` descriptor in the full lockup.

## Typography

- Display / wordmark: **Playfair Display SemiBold (60)** — `0.14em` tracking, uppercase.
- Descriptor / UI: **Inter Medium (50)** — `0.46em` tracking, uppercase.

## Clearspace & minimum sizes

- Clearspace: `25%` of the mark's height on every side.
- Full mark: minimum `32px` digital / `8mm` print.
- App tile: use below `32px`; it is tuned for `16px`.
- Horizontal lockup: minimum `220px` digital / `50mm` print.

## Rules

- Do not stretch, rotate, outline or recolour the mark.
- Do not add ticks, hands, taglines or badges inside the bezel.
- Use cream on ink or ink on cream; avoid mid-tone on mid-tone.
- Keep the champagne dot. For one-colour printing, use the mono files.

Website integration is theme-adaptive: the mark and wordmark inherit the interface text colour, while the dot stays champagne.
